# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Repobox a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, threats, publishing private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. Report abuse privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact channels. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where possible and may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this code.
